# Sum up 24 November's world news in one image

Entry for the make.fun bounty `news-image-20261124`.

`main.py` copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`. The image is a
newspaper front-page style editorial collage of Tuesday 24 November 2026:

- **Huawei verdict** — a US federal jury in Brooklyn convicted Huawei on
  10 of 12 criminal counts (bank fraud, sanctions violations, trade-secret
  theft conspiracy, money laundering).
- **Nvidia swings** — after Jensen Huang's reinstatement sent the stock up
  31.48% on 23 November, it gave back 5.86% on 24 November.
- **Riyadh tower hit** — a Houthi drone struck the PIF tower in Riyadh for
  the second time in a week, injuring 11 people.
- **Shenzhou-24 launches** — China's Shenzhou-24 mission carrying the
  first foreign astronaut to visit the Tiangong station, Pakistan's
  Muhammad Zeeshan Ali.

Image specs: PNG, 1024 × 1024, under 1 MiB.
